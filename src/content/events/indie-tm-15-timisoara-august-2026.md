Two weeks after a photograph tried to become a living room, Indie TM #15 returned to one founder and one product. [Andrei Gudiu](/people/andrei-gudiu) brought [ACTE123](https://www.acte123.ro). The full first-person story is in [his journey](/journeys/andrei-acte123). This recap is what the room did to the plan.

![ACTE123 homepage](/screenshots/acte123.png)

## What Andrei put on the table

Motorcycles kept sending Andrei to vehicle registration. On 26 October 2024 he read a board where several cases shared seven requirements.

A procedure plus a scenario yields a versioned plan. ACTE123 asks the questions and turns the answers into steps, requirements, fees, and forms.

The Dumbrăvița story showed why the questions matter. One counter stamped a translated contract. The next required an authorized translation and the English original. One missing question caused another trip.

## The room asked how the systems connect

Timișoara has a portal. Lugoj uses ConnectX. The room argued that the hard part is the walk between institutions, not the login inside one portal. Access to RO-ID sounded closer to PSD2 due diligence than to opening another form. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk) offered the useful comparison. It asks questions and produces a printable plan, even when the final step still happens in person.

Then the roast started, and it was about the homepage, not the JSON.

## This is several companies

The room's diagnosis was rude and useful. ACTE123 is a personal vault, an org procedure tool, a shareable profile, a marketplace, a swipe UI, a voice counter, a PDF mapper, a civic campaign, and an SEO publisher. That is too many sentences for one landing page and too many buyers for one sales call. The room wanted Andrei to pick the org that would write its own procedure. Kindergartens, registration firms, apartment admins, or a workshop could be that customer. Do not pitch ten solutions to a person who only needed a checklist.

The kindergarten that belongs to someone's sister became the concrete objection. Inspectors want a paper file. Printing from the system looks like extra work. Until you can name the pain that makes her pay, you do not have a B2B product. You have a relative being polite.

:::advice{slug="do-not-sell-ten-products-as-one-homepage" category="business" person="andrei-gudiu" title="Do not sell ten products as one homepage"}
The room looked at ACTE123's vault, org SOP builder, shareable profiles, marketplace, swipe mode, counter chatbot, PDF mapper, and civic traffic goal, and heard several companies sharing a compiler. That compiler is an asset. The homepage is not allowed to list every asset. A buyer cannot tell whether they are paying for storage, for a kindergarten enrollment sheet, or for a movement to simplify the state. Split the pitch until one job is left, usually the org that already runs a procedure, and let the other jobs become modules once someone has paid. If you need a breath to explain what you sell, you are not selling yet. You are touring the architecture.
:::

:::advice{slug="charge-the-firm-that-already-keeps-the-file" category="business" person="andrei-gudiu" title="Charge the firm that already keeps the file"}
ACTE123 had 63 users and none paid. The room's preferred customer was not "everyone who hates bureaucracy." It was the registration firm, the kindergarten, the administrator, or another office that already collects documents and cares when a file is wrong. Those buyers define their own procedures. They can import an existing procedure with a model and spend less time building. The consumer vault can stay free while the organization pays for the workflow. Roviniete.ro turning over 69 million lei proves paperwork budgets exist. It does not prove a consumer will pay for Plus. Price the person who fails an inspection when the folder is incomplete.
:::

## Call fifty kindergartens before you add another mode

Validation advice was unfashionably analog. Record a two-minute video for kindergartens. Call fifty of them in Timiș. Do not lead with the solution. Ask what hurts. The sister who will not switch is the interview, not the objection to talk around. A model that turns an existing paper procedure into a form matters only after someone has said they want that form.

Andrei already wanted a human who has worked registrations and can type. The room treated that hire as more important than another model. A model can draft much of a procedure. The practitioner must finish the part that sends people home.

:::advice{slug="call-fifty-kindergartens-before-another-mode" category="distribution" person="andrei-gudiu" title="Call fifty kindergartens before you add another mode"}
Faced with Tinder swipe, a rude counter bot, guest sessions, and a civic traffic goal, the room prescribed a smaller loop. Make a two-minute video for one type of organization, then call fifty of them in Timiș. The kindergarten that prefers a paper dossier for inspectors is not a UX nit. It is the buying criterion. If printing from the system costs her time, she will not pay to save parents time. That answer only shows up on the phone. Shipping another navigation mode postpones the only number that matters, how many of those fifty ask for a second conversation. Pick one customer that owns a procedure and exhaust the list before you invent a new way to tick a checkbox.
:::

## Write Sardinia, then hope the models copy you

Marketing, the question from the start of the night, finally got a plan. Programmatic pages should cover long-tail procedures such as registering a car brought from Sardinia, not "car registration" as a category. Watch the news for the next institution mess and publish a guide while Google is still hungry. The prize above both is to become the source language models cite when someone asks what papers they need. The plan is the article. The article is the plan.

It is also a way to stay B2C forever. The room let the SEO sit next to the B2B call list and did not pretend they are the same calendar.

:::advice{slug="write-the-sardinia-article" category="seo" person="andrei-gudiu" title="Write the Sardinia article, not the generic registration guide"}
Andrei's SEO sketch was specific enough to steal. People do not search "Romanian administrative compiler." They search a situation, a car brought from Italy, from Sardinia, a child passport, a rent declaration they cannot find in the ANAF menu. A page that names that situation can win a long-tail query the ministry page is too generic to take. The same logic extends to publishing a guide when an institution is in the news, while the index is still moving, and to becoming the citation ChatGPT repeats. Programmatic SEO is not a thousand thin pages. It is one honest plan per messy case, generated from the same JSON the user would walk. If your product is a recipe, the URL should be the recipe. If the URL is a category, you are competing with the ministry.
:::

## Fifteen editions in

Edition fourteen asked who pays for a sofa that exists only in a photograph. Edition fifteen made the room ask who pays for this queue before Andrei builds another feature. The answer was one customer who already keeps a file, fifty calls before another mode, and a homepage that sells one job. The afterparty invitation sent us to Check-Inn to keep talking about the jump from zero to one. See you at the sixteenth.
